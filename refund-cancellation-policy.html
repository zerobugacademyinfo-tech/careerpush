Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [date to be added on publish]

1. Billing frequency

CareerPush plans (Silver, Gold, Platinum) are billed monthly at the price shown on our Pricing page at the time you subscribe.

2. Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically each month unless cancelled before the renewal date.

3. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancelling stops future billing — you'll keep access to your plan's features for the rest of the period you've already paid for, and it won't renew after that.

4. Refunds

We do not provide partial or prorated refunds for the current billing period once it has started. If you're charged in error — for example, a duplicate charge or an incorrect amount — contact us and we'll correct it.

5. Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we'll let you know and give you a reasonable window to update your payment details before your access is affected.

6. Service suspension

We may pause active support on your account if a renewal payment isn't resolved after that window, or in cases of misuse inconsistent with our Terms of Service. We'll always tell you why before doing so.

7. How to cancel

To cancel, message us using the details on our Contact page.
