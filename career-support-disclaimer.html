Career Support Disclaimer

1. Not a recruitment agency or guarantee

CareerPush is a subscription career-support service, not a recruitment agency, staffing firm, or placement guarantee. We do not guarantee interviews, job offers, employment, or any specific salary outcome for any candidate on any plan.

2. No confidential or insider employer information

We do not have confidential or privileged access to any employer's hiring process. Any company or interview context we share is gathered through legitimate, candidate-authorized professional interactions and publicly available information — never through confidential channels.

3. Candidate-authorized methods only

Application assistance, profile setup, and outreach are performed using candidate-authorized, platform-permitted methods. We do not use scraping, browser automation, or unauthorized automated activity on any job platform, and we never ask for your job portal passwords. If a platform's terms don't permit a particular workflow, we don't perform it.

4. Outcomes depend on factors we don't control

Whether you receive an interview, an offer, or a role depends on many factors outside CareerPush's control — including your qualifications, experience, role availability, employer requirements, market conditions, employer decisions, and your own interview performance. Our role is to support and assist your job search, not to control its outcome.

5. After-joining support

After-joining support (available on Gold and Platinum plans) is guidance and support for your own transition into a new role — it does not extend to resolving an employer's internal HR, project, or workplace issues on your behalf.
