What's Included

Every CareerPush plan builds on the one before it — Gold includes everything in Silver, Platinum includes everything in Gold.

Profile & Resume Setup

Start strong with a resume and online presence built to actually pass the first filter — an ATS-focused resume, not just something that looks good to a human reader.

How it works: you share your resume and background with us over WhatsApp — no portal logins, ever. We rebuild or refine your resume with ATS-focused formatting, and set up or optimize your profiles across major job platforms (Naukri, LinkedIn, Shine, Monster, Foundit, and more — specific methods can vary by each platform's current rules).

  • ATS-focused resume
  • Profile setup across major job platforms
  • Gold+ Monthly resume review
  • Platinum On-demand resume tweaks

Job Matching & Application Assistance

Stop refreshing job portals yourself. We match your profile against relevant openings and handle the applying, using candidate-authorized, platform-permitted methods.

How it works: WhatsApp alerts land when a role matches your profile, and applications go out using methods each platform actually permits. Gold and Platinum candidates get priority — their profile is worked ahead of standard-tier candidates.

  • WhatsApp job alerts matched to your profile
  • Application assistance, candidate-authorized methods
  • Gold+ Priority matching & faster follow-up
  • Platinum Top-priority matching & same-day follow-up

Platinum — Premium Career Support

Everything in Gold, plus direct 1:1 time with a career specialist.

Salary & Offer Guidance

Personalized guidance on evaluating and negotiating an offer once one comes in — what to weigh beyond the number, and how to have that conversation with an employer.
