What's Included

Every CareerPush plan builds on the one before it — Gold includes everything in Silver, Platinum includes everything in Gold. This is the core of what you're actually paying for: the ongoing work, not a one-time document.

Job Matching & Application Assistance

Stop refreshing job portals yourself. We match your profile against relevant openings and handle the applying, using candidate-authorized, platform-permitted methods — every day, not once.

How it works: WhatsApp alerts land when a role matches your profile, and applications go out using methods each platform actually permits. Gold and Platinum candidates get priority — their profile is worked ahead of standard-tier candidates.

  • WhatsApp job alerts matched to your profile
  • Application assistance, candidate-authorized methods
  • Gold+ Priority matching & faster follow-up
  • Platinum Top-priority matching & same-day follow-up

Progress Updates

No wondering where things stand. You get a real update from a real person, on a schedule matched to your plan.

  • Silver Bi-weekly WhatsApp updates
  • Gold Weekly WhatsApp updates
  • Platinum Twice-weekly updates, or on request

But building a resume isn't hard to find elsewhere — plenty of tools do that. What's harder to sustain alone is everything above: consistent applying, real follow-up, and being ready when the interview call comes. That's where CareerPush actually spends its effort.

  • ATS-focused resume & profile setup, included in every plan
  • Gold+ Monthly resume review
  • Platinum On-demand resume tweaks

Platinum — Premium Career Support

Everything in Gold, plus direct 1:1 time with a career specialist.

Salary & Offer Guidance

Personalized guidance on evaluating and negotiating an offer once one comes in — what to weigh beyond the number, and how to have that conversation with an employer.

Employer Introduction — Where Available

Where a legitimate recruiter or employer relationship exists, we may facilitate an introduction. This isn't guaranteed for every role or company — it depends on what relationships genuinely exist at the time.

Monthly 1:1 Career Call

A direct call each month — not just a status update, but a conversation about career direction, not only the immediate job search.

Why Platinum Is Capped

Platinum is limited to 5 active candidates per specialist. This is a real operational limit, not manufactured urgency — it's what it actually takes to keep the 1:1 attention above genuine, not diluted.

Scope of services: CareerPush strengthens your job search process and visibility. We do not guarantee interviews, job offers, employment, or any specific salary outcome. Hiring decisions are made independently by employers.

Service applicability: results depend on factors including your qualifications, experience, role availability, employer requirements, market conditions, and interview performance.

Candidate-authorized methods only: application assistance uses candidate-authorized, platform-permitted methods. We never request job portal passwords, and don't perform any workflow a platform's terms don't allow.

Billing: monthly, cancel anytime. The current billing period isn't refunded, but there's no long-term lock-in. Full terms: CareerPush Terms of Service & Refund and Cancellation Policy.
